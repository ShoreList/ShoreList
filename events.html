Before you go

Wilmington NC events: common questions

What is happening in Wilmington NC this weekend?

The weekend row near the top of this page lists the events running this weekend, and the weekend guide has the full Friday to Sunday list.

Are there free events in Wilmington NC?

Yes. Listings that organizers mark as free carry a Free tag, and the Free quick filter in the calendar shows only those. Confirm with the organizer before you go, since details can change.

Which areas does the events calendar cover?

Downtown Wilmington, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach. Each has its own page: downtown Wilmington, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach.

Where do the event listings come from?

Every listing links to an official or organizer page, and the calendar is refreshed daily. Dates, times and tickets can change, so use the organizer link to confirm before you plan around an event.

Are there holiday and Christmas events in Wilmington NC?

The calendar includes holiday listings during the season, and the Seasonal highlights section below adds holiday outings that are not dated listings. For dates by month, see the events by month page.

Where do I park for events in downtown Wilmington?

The City of Wilmington runs five downtown parking decks, and on-street meters are enforced Monday to Saturday from 9 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. Read the Wilmington parking guide for the decks and the beach towns, and allow extra time on event weekends.

  • Interactive Downtown Map ↗

    Every parking deck, surface lot, EV charger, restroom, trolley stop, and water taxi downtown, searchable by destination. Run by Wilmington Downtown, Inc.

    Feed the meter from your phone, and top it up from your seat when the encore runs long. Works at metered street parking throughout downtown.

  • Know Before You Go

    Meters are enforced Mon to Sat, 9:00 AM to 6:30 PM at $2.50/hr. Evenings after 6:30 and Sundays are free street parking, which suits Pavilion shows and ghost walks.

Your Complete Guide to Things to Do in Wilmington, NC

Wilmington, NC is a coastal city with a busy events calendar. From holiday tree lightings, drone shows and boat flotillas on the Cape Fear to concerts at the Wilson Center and Live Oak Bank Pavilion, and beloved annual festivals, there is genuinely something to do in Wilmington every week of the year.

The Greenfield Lake Amphitheater offers intimate lakeside shows with artists like Blackberry Smoke, Rhiannon Giddens, and Band of Horses. The Wilson Center at CFCC brings touring artists downtown. And Dead Crow Comedy Room and Seven Mile Post keep local entertainment alive year-round.
