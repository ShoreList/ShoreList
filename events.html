Plan Your Visit — Downtown Toolkit

Park Smart. Arrive Happy.

Heading downtown for a show, a holiday event, or a ghost walk? There’s no on-site parking at Live Oak Bank Pavilion — but downtown has plenty of decks, lots, and metered street parking. These tools make it painless.

Interactive Downtown Map ↗
Every parking deck, surface lot, EV charger, restroom, trolley stop, and water taxi downtown — searchable by destination. Run by Wilmington Downtown, Inc.
PayByPhone Parking App ↗
Feed the meter from your phone — and top it up from your seat when the encore runs long. Works at metered street parking throughout downtown.
Know Before You Go
Meters are enforced Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM–6:30 PM at $2.50/hr. Evenings after 6:30 and Sundays are free street parking — perfect timing for Pavilion shows and ghost walks.
