Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 13, 2026 · Effective: May 13, 2026

ShoreList LLC (“ShoreList,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website located at shorelist.net (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit or use our Site.

By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Who We Are

ShoreList LLC is a North Carolina limited liability company headquartered in Wilmington, NC. We operate an online marketplace connecting visitors with local experience operators — tours, surf lessons, fishing charters, nature walks, and more — in the Wilmington, NC coastal region.

Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit the Site, we automatically receive certain technical information including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent on the Site. This information helps us understand how the Site is used and improve the experience.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or knowingly retain sensitive personal data including Social Security numbers (except Tax ID from operators for IRS reporting), government-issued ID numbers, health information, or biometric data. We do not collect information from children under 13.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To process and confirm bookings — your name, email, phone, and booking details are shared with the operator you book with
  • To process payments — payment data is sent to Stripe for processing. ShoreList does not store payment card data on our servers
  • To send booking confirmations and receipts — via email immediately after checkout
  • To send launch notifications — if you joined our waitlist, we will email you when bookings go live. You can unsubscribe at any time
  • To improve the platform — aggregate, anonymized usage data helps us understand what works and what doesn't
  • To prevent fraud and maintain security
  • To comply with legal obligations — including tax reporting, dispute resolution, and law enforcement requests where required

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent, trade, or sell your personal information to third-party marketers, data brokers, or advertisers — ever.

Payments & Stripe

All payment processing on ShoreList is handled by Stripe, Inc., a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified third-party payment processor. When you enter payment information at checkout, that information is transmitted directly to Stripe using encrypted connections (TLS/SSL). ShoreList never sees, receives, or stores your full credit card number, CVV, or payment account credentials.

Stripe processes your payment, charges the applicable amount, and remits the operator's portion to their account. Stripe retains transaction data in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

Cancellations & Refunds

Guests who cancel at least 24 hours before their scheduled experience are entitled to a full refund processed through Stripe back to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your card issuer.

ShoreList does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The Site is not directed to children under 13. Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption on all pages of the Site (HTTPS enforced)
  • Stripe's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processing — we never handle raw payment card data
  • Netlify's secure hosting infrastructure for platform reliability and security
  • Limited data access — only authorized ShoreList personnel can access booking data

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you promptly in accordance with applicable law.

Data Retention

  • Booking records — retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes in compliance with IRS requirements
  • Waitlist email addresses — retained until the launch date and then either converted to active user accounts or deleted within 90 days unless you opt in to further communications
  • Operator application data — retained for the duration of the operator relationship plus 3 years
  • Web server logs — retained for 90 days then automatically purged

Cookies & Tracking

ShoreList uses a minimal cookie footprint. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking technologies.

Cookies We Use

  • Session cookies — to maintain your browsing session as you navigate the Site. These expire when you close your browser.
  • Stripe cookies — set by Stripe during checkout for fraud prevention and security. Required for payment processing to function.
  • Google Analytics — we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site in aggregate. Google Analytics is configured to anonymize IP addresses. You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Why We Do Not Display a Cookie Banner

The European Union's GDPR and the United Kingdom's PECR require explicit consent for non-essential cookies. ShoreList does not deploy non-essential cookies (no advertising trackers, no social media pixels, no cross-site tracking). Strictly necessary cookies (session, security) do not require consent under GDPR Article 5(3). Analytics cookies use anonymized IP addresses and aggregate reporting only, falling under our legitimate interest basis. We will introduce a cookie consent mechanism if our cookie usage materially expands in the future.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so may affect the functionality of the checkout process.
