Section 3

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To process and confirm bookings — your name, email, phone, and booking details are shared with the operator you book with so they can prepare for your experience
  • To process payments — payment data is sent to Stripe for processing. ShoreList does not store payment card data on our servers
  • To send booking confirmations and receipts — via email immediately after checkout
  • To send launch notifications — if you joined our waitlist, we will email you when bookings go live on June 1st. You can unsubscribe from these emails at any time
  • To communicate with operators — operator applicants will receive onboarding communication from ShoreList staff
  • To improve the platform — aggregate, anonymized usage data helps us understand what works and what doesn't
  • To prevent fraud and maintain security — we may use information to detect and prevent fraudulent transactions or abuse of the platform
  • To comply with legal obligations — including tax reporting, dispute resolution, and law enforcement requests where required
We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent, trade, or sell your personal information to third-party marketers, data brokers, or advertisers — ever.
Section 4

Payments & Stripe

All payment processing on ShoreList is handled by Stripe, Inc., a third-party payment processor. When you enter payment information at checkout, that information is transmitted directly to Stripe using encrypted connections (TLS/SSL). ShoreList never sees, receives, or stores your full credit card number, CVV, or payment account credentials.

Stripe processes your payment, charges the applicable amount, and remits the operator's portion to their account. Stripe retains transaction data in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

ShoreList retains a record of completed transactions for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution purposes. This record includes the booking amount, date, operator, and your email address — not your payment card details.

Cancellations & Refunds

Guests who cancel at least 24 hours before their scheduled experience are entitled to a full refund processed through Stripe back to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your card issuer. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the experience are subject to the operator's individual cancellation policy.

Weather, Cancellations & Force Majeure

Force Majeure Events

Neither ShoreList nor any operator shall be liable for cancellations, delays, or failure to perform caused by circumstances beyond their reasonable control ("Force Majeure Events"), including but not limited to:

  • Hurricanes, tropical storms, tornadoes, flooding, lightning, or other severe weather events
  • Acts of God or nature, including earthquakes, wildfires, or extreme weather
  • Epidemic, pandemic, or public health emergencies declared by a government authority
  • Government orders, mandatory evacuations, curfews, or beach closures
  • Power outages or utility failures affecting the operator's ability to deliver the experience
  • Civil unrest, strikes, or other events outside the operator's control

Guest Refunds for Force Majeure

If a booked experience is cancelled due to a Force Majeure Event, guests will receive a full refund of all amounts paid. Refunds will be processed through Stripe within 5–10 business days. ShoreList will notify affected guests by email as promptly as practicable after a cancellation decision is made.

Operator-Initiated Cancellations

If an operator cancels a confirmed booking for reasons other than Force Majeure — including illness, scheduling conflicts, equipment failure, or staffing issues — the guest will receive a full refund. Operators who cancel confirmed bookings without cause may be subject to suspension or removal from the ShoreList platform at ShoreList's sole discretion.

Weather Policy for Outdoor Activities

For outdoor activities affected by weather short of a Force Majeure Event, the cancellation decision rests with the operator based on their professional judgment and safety standards. Guests who are concerned about weather should contact ShoreList at shorelistwilmington@gmail.com at least 24 hours before their scheduled experience.

Section 15

Tax Reporting & 1099-K

This section applies to operators who receive payments through the ShoreList platform.

IRS Form 1099-K

ShoreList LLC is a third-party payment settlement entity under IRS regulations. As required by federal law, ShoreList will issue IRS Form 1099-K to any operator whose gross payments processed through ShoreList meet or exceed the applicable reporting threshold in any calendar year. As of 2024, the IRS threshold is $600 in gross payments. Operators should consult a tax professional regarding their specific obligations.

W-9 Requirement

Before ShoreList processes any payout to an operator, the operator must provide a completed IRS Form W-9 (or W-8BEN for non-US persons), including their legal name, business name (if applicable), address, and taxpayer identification number (EIN or SSN). Operators provide their EIN or tax identification number during the application process.

Failure to provide a valid W-9 may result in ShoreList withholding 24% backup withholding from operator payouts as required by IRS regulations. This withheld amount will be remitted to the IRS on the operator's behalf.

Operator Tax Responsibilities

Operators are solely responsible for reporting their ShoreList income on their own federal and state tax returns. ShoreList does not provide tax advice. ShoreList recommends that all operators consult a qualified tax professional regarding their obligations, including but not limited to income tax, self-employment tax, and NC state tax obligations.

Stripe and Payment Processing

Payouts are processed through Stripe Connect. Operators may also receive tax documentation directly from Stripe in connection with their Stripe account. See stripe.com/docs/connect/tax-reporting for more information on Stripe's tax reporting practices.

For operators: Your EIN or SSN provided during signup is used solely for IRS tax reporting purposes. It is stored securely and never displayed publicly on the ShoreList platform.
