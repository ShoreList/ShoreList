America’s Film City.

300 years of history on the Cape Fear River. The largest intact collection of antebellum architecture in NC. The biggest film studio east of Hollywood. Ghost tours that have been terrifying visitors for decades. The Battleship North Carolina sitting 400 feet from the Riverwalk. There is no city in the South quite like Wilmington.
