Wilmington Railroad Museum

Climb aboard a real 1911 steam locomotive, walk through a life-size caboose, and watch 20+ model trains run through a 1,600 sq ft layout. Housed in an authentic 1883 freight warehouse at the north end of downtown, this 501(c)(3) nonprofit has preserved Wilmington’s Atlantic Coast Line railroad story since 1979. Indoors and fun in any weather, and a family-friendly stop downtown.

Quick answers

Downtown Wilmington: quick answers

Downtown Wilmington centers on the Riverwalk, which stretches nearly two miles along the Cape Fear River from Nun Street to the Isabella Holmes Bridge. The City of Wilmington runs five parking decks, and on-street meters are enforced Monday to Saturday from 9 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.

How long is the Wilmington Riverwalk?

Nearly two miles, from Nun Street to the Isabella Holmes Bridge, per the visitors bureau. Riverfront Park, a 6.63 acre waterfront park, anchors the north end.

Where do I park for the Riverwalk and downtown tours?

The city lists five decks: Skyline, Convention Center, River Place, 2nd Street and Market Street. Pick the closest to your meeting point. Addresses are in the parking guide.

Are downtown Wilmington parking meters free on Sunday?

Yes. Per the City of Wilmington, meters are enforced Monday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., and are free after 6:30 p.m. and all day Sunday. Read the sign at the meter, since rules on a block can differ.

Can you fish from the Riverwalk?

Conlon Pier is described by the visitors bureau as the only place along the Riverwalk where fishing is allowed. For guided trips, see fishing charters and river cruises.

Sources, checked November 2026: Wilmington and Beaches: guide to the Riverwalk; City of Wilmington: parking. Rules, hours and fees change, so confirm before you go.
