Frequently Asked Questions

ShoreList is Wilmington's first hyperlocal experience booking platform — built specifically for the Wilmington & the Beaches area. We connect visitors and locals with vetted, locally-owned operators offering everything from ghost tours and surf lessons to kayak tours, film location experiences, and coastal nature walks.
You pay the full price at booking — no surprises, no hidden fees. Secure checkout through Stripe takes under 60 seconds. Your booking is instantly confirmed and the operator is notified.
Every ShoreList operator is personally reviewed before going live. We verify their business license, confirm they carry appropriate liability insurance, and require them to sign a formal operator agreement.
Cancel at least 24 hours before your experience and you receive a full refund, no questions asked. Weather-related cancellations always receive a full refund.
