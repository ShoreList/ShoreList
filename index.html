Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

ShoreList is Wilmington’s first hyperlocal experience booking platform — built specifically for the Wilmington & the Beaches area. We connect visitors and locals with vetted, locally-owned operators offering everything from ghost tours and surf lessons to kayak tours, film location experiences, and coastal nature walks. Think of us as the Wilmington-only alternative to Viator or GetYourGuide — but with operators you can actually trust and experiences that are genuinely local.
ShoreList officially launches on June 1st, 2026. Guests on the waitlist get early access and members-only launch offers before the platform opens to the public. Operators are onboarding now — with 4 live and more coming before launch day.
There is no upfront cost to book. ShoreList uses a small refundable $25 security deposit to confirm your reservation — this guarantees your spot and holds the operator accountable. The deposit is credited toward your experience or fully refunded. You will never be charged hidden fees or surprise costs.
Every ShoreList operator is personally reviewed before going live. We verify their business license, confirm they carry appropriate liability insurance, and require them to sign a formal operator agreement. We only work with operators we’d personally recommend to a friend visiting Wilmington for the first time.
ShoreList covers 18 categories of local coastal experiences: ghost & history tours, surf lessons, film & TV location tours, kayak & paddleboard tours, sunset sails, fishing charters, nature walks (including wild Venus flytraps), e-bike rentals, beach photography, dolphin watch cruises, and more. Every category features Wilmington-area operators only — no national chains.
Cancellation policies vary by operator and are clearly displayed before you confirm any booking. Most operators offer full refunds with 24–48 hours notice. The $25 deposit is always refundable unless you cancel within 24 hours of your experience. Weather-related cancellations are handled directly by the operator and ShoreList.
