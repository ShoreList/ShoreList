Questions

How do I get the files?

After you pay on Stripe’s secure checkout you come back to a ShoreList page with a download button for each print you bought. Keep that page open until you have saved the files.

What can I do with a print?

Print it for your own home or office, or as a gift for one person. You cannot resell the file or the print, or put the art on products for sale.

Which size should I print?

Page 1 is 18x24 inches and page 2 is 24x36 inches. Pick the page that matches your frame. Most print shops accept the PDF as it is; ask for no scaling and a matte or semi-gloss paper.

Is anything missing from my download?

Email rj@shorelist.net and we will help.

Is my payment secure?

Payment is handled by Stripe. ShoreList never sees or stores your card number.
