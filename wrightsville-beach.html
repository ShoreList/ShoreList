Wrightsville Beach, NC
The Premier Beach.

Consistently ranked among the best beaches in the Southeast for good reason. Clear water, reliable surf, Johnnie Mercer’s Pier, Banks Channel paddling, and Masonboro Island — the last undeveloped barrier island in NC — right across the ICW. This is the Wilmington beach that raises the bar.

Wildlife & Dolphin Tours
Masonboro Island is NC’s largest undeveloped barrier island, sitting just across the ICW from Wrightsville Beach. Bottlenose dolphins are abundant year-round in these waters. Nesting shorebirds, loggerhead sea turtles, and total natural beauty. Guided eco-tours departing from Wrightsville Beach.
