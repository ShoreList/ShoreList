Quick answers

Wrightsville Beach: quick answers

Wrightsville Beach is about 20 minutes east of downtown Wilmington and about 10 miles from Wilmington International Airport, according to the visitors bureau. Beach access has no charge, but parking meters run daily from March 1 to October 31, and pets are not allowed on the beach strand from April 1 to September 30.

Is parking free at Wrightsville Beach?

Not during the paid season. Per the visitors bureau, meters are enforced every day from March 1 to October 31, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., and some meters until 8 p.m. The town has over 1,600 metered public spaces and takes payment at pay stations or by Text2Park. See the parking guide.

Are dogs allowed on Wrightsville Beach?

Leashed pets are allowed on public property all year except the beach strand from April 1 to September 30, per the town pet page. Rules can change, so call the Park Ranger Office at (910) 239-1751 before you go.

When are lifeguards on duty at Wrightsville Beach?

Every day from Memorial weekend through Labor Day weekend, according to the visitors bureau. Check the live beach and surf report before you swim.

Is there a fee to get onto Wrightsville Beach?

No. The visitors bureau says there is no charge to access the beach. Costs come from parking, rentals and tours, such as surf lessons and paddleboard rentals.
