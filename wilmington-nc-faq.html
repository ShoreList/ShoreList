The basics

What is Wilmington, NC known for?

Wilmington is known for its riverfront historic district and Riverwalk on the Cape Fear River, the Battleship North Carolina, a long film and TV history, spring azaleas, and three nearby beach towns: Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach.

Downtown guide Things to do

How far is Wilmington from Raleigh, Charlotte and Myrtle Beach?

Wilmington sits at the eastern end of Interstate 40. Raleigh is roughly a two hour drive, Myrtle Beach about an hour and a half, and Charlotte around three and a half hours. Times vary with traffic, especially on summer weekends.

Trip planning guide

Is downtown Wilmington walkable?

Yes. The historic district, the Riverwalk, restaurants, shops and most downtown tours are within a compact, walkable area. You will want a car or a ride to reach the beaches.

Things to do

What are the best things to do in Wilmington with kids?

Popular family stops include the Battleship North Carolina, the Wilmington Railroad Museum, the Children's Museum of Wilmington, the Carolina Beach Boardwalk in season, and the beach itself. Check each attraction's site for current hours before you go.

Museums and attractions Railroad Museum

What can you do in Wilmington for free?

Walk the Riverwalk and the historic district, spend a day on the beach (parking may cost), hike the trails at Carolina Beach State Park, and visit the grounds of Fort Fisher State Historic Site. Many community events and markets are free as well.

Events Nature walks

What is there to do in Wilmington at night?

Downtown has restaurants, rooftop bars, live music and ghost walks after dark. In summer the Carolina Beach Boardwalk is lively in the evenings. Larger concerts play venues such as Live Oak Bank Pavilion, Greenfield Lake Amphitheater and the Wilson Center.

What TV shows and movies were filmed in Wilmington?

Wilmington has hosted film and TV production for decades. One Tree Hill and Dawson's Creek were filmed here, and more recent productions include The Summer I Turned Pretty. Location tours visit many recognizable spots.

Film and TV location tours

Can you tour the Battleship North Carolina?

Yes. The World War II battleship is moored across the Cape Fear River from downtown and is open for self-guided tours. Check the Battleship's official site for hours and ticket prices.

Museums and attractions

Where can I see Venus flytraps?

Carolina Beach State Park has a short Flytrap Trail where the plants grow wild. They are small and protected by law, so look closely, stay on the trail and never pick or dig them.

Nature walks Carolina Beach guide

When are the big festivals in Wilmington?

The North Carolina Azalea Festival is held each spring, usually in April, and Riverfest is held downtown in the fall. Summer brings fireworks and boardwalk events at Carolina Beach, and the holiday season has parades, markets and a flotilla. Confirm dates on the events page.

Beaches

Which beach is closest to downtown Wilmington?

Wrightsville Beach is the closest, about 20 minutes east of downtown. Carolina Beach is about 30 minutes south, and Kure Beach is just past it on the same island.

Wrightsville Beach Carolina Beach Kure Beach

Do you have to pay to park at the beach?

Often, yes. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach each set their own paid parking seasons, hours and rates, usually paid by app or kiosk. Check the town's website before you go and arrive early on summer weekends.

Trip planning guide

Are dogs allowed on the beaches near Wilmington?

Rules differ by town and by season, and some beaches restrict dogs during the warm months or require a leash. Check the current rules for the town you are visiting before you bring a dog onto the sand.

Is it safe to swim at Wrightsville, Carolina and Kure Beach?

Lifeguards are on duty seasonally at many beach accesses. Swim near a lifeguard, obey the flags posted on the beach, and learn how to spot and escape a rip current. ShoreList shows live surf conditions and an estimated flag, but the flags and lifeguards on the beach always come first.

Live beach and surf report

Where is the best place to watch the sunset?

Downtown, the Riverwalk faces west over the Cape Fear River. At Wrightsville Beach, the sound side along Banks Channel glows at sunset. A sunset cruise or sail is another favorite.

River cruises Sailboat rides

Can beginners surf at Wrightsville Beach?

Yes. Wrightsville Beach is a popular place to learn, and local instructors run lessons for first-timers when conditions allow. Check the live surf report before you book.

Surf lessons

Is there a ferry from Fort Fisher to Southport?

Yes. The state-run Fort Fisher to Southport ferry crosses the Cape Fear River in roughly 35 minutes and carries cars and walk-on passengers. Check the NCDOT ferry schedule and fares before you go.

Kure Beach and Fort Fisher guide

Can you rent golf carts at Carolina Beach and Kure Beach?

Yes. Street-legal golf carts are a popular way to get around Pleasure Island. Drivers must be licensed and follow the same road rules as cars.
