Questions

What can I buy in the ShoreList Shop?

Embroidered and printed apparel, home goods, beach gear and can coolers, designed for Wilmington, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach.

Where can I find a gift from Wilmington NC?

The gift guide sorts pieces from the shop by who is getting them, from can coolers and mugs to embroidered crewnecks and beach-day gear.

How does checkout work?

Pieces from the ShoreList Shop go in your bag and check out together on one secure page. One piece, the organic cotton market tote, opens on its own page and checks out there, separately from your bag, so it ships as its own order.

How long does an order take?

Every piece is made after you order it, so nothing sits in a warehouse. Most orders ship within 2 to 7 business days of being submitted, then delivery time applies. The shipping method, cost and estimated delivery for your address are shown at checkout, and you will get a tracking link by email when your order ships.

What is your returns policy?

Because each piece is made for you, we do not take returns for a change of mind or a different size. Please check the size guide first. If something arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or in the wrong size, write to rj@shorelist.net within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you. Full details are on the delivery and returns page.

How do I find my size?

Our size guide has measurements for the apparel. Open any piece, then choose your size on the product.

Are the product images photographs?

Some of the styled scenes on this page are generated images that show how a piece looks in use. They are not photographs of real places. The studio images show the actual product.

Is the shop affiliated with Airlie Gardens or the places on your products?

No. ShoreList is an independent Wilmington company. The Airlie Oak and the other landmarks and places on our products are named only to describe what is shown. ShoreList is unofficial and not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Airlie Gardens, New Hanover County or any other place or organization shown.
