Questions

How does checkout work?

Pieces from ShoreList Shop go in your bag and check out together on Shopify. A few pieces are sold through our second store, ShoreList Gear. They open on their own page and check out there, separately from your bag. Our print partners make and ship each order, so pieces from the two stores can arrive separately.

Is everything made to order?

Yes. Items are made after you order, so allow production time before shipping.

How do I find my size?

Our size guide has measurements for the apparel. Open it from any piece, then choose your size on the product.

What if something arrives wrong?

Everything is made to order, so we cannot take returns for change of mind or size preference. If an item arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or in the wrong size, we replace it at no cost when you report it within 30 days of delivery. The delivery and returns page has the full details.
