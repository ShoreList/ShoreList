Questions

Who ships my order?

Our print partner makes and ships each order. Pick your color and size here, add pieces to your bag, then pay once at our secure Shopify checkout. A few pieces are sold through our second store and open on their own page.

Is everything made to order?

Yes. Items are printed or engraved after you order, so allow production time before shipping.

What if something arrives wrong?

Sales are final, but if a file is broken or an item isn't as described, we'll fix it or refund within 14 days. See the refund policy at checkout.
