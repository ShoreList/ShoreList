Learn to surf

Start with a lesson, not a purchase

In summer the waves here are usually small and the bottom is sand, which is a friendly combination for a first try. A lesson includes a board and starts on the sand before you paddle out.

Rent before you buy

Most people only need a board for an afternoon. Start with a lesson or a rental from a local operator and decide later whether to buy.

1

You learn what fits

Board length, width and thickness change how stable a board feels. An hour on the water tells you more than a spec sheet.

2

Nothing to carry or store

A board or a hard kayak needs a way onto the car and a place to live at home. A rental goes back at the end of the day.

3

You find out if it sticks

Plenty of people love one lesson and leave it there. Buy once you know you will use it.

Ready to buy? Our guides explain what to look for. ShoreList does not sell surfboards, kayaks or wakeboards. For those, visit a local surf shop. They can fit you in person and they know these breaks.

Surf questions

Short answers, linked to the pages that go deeper.

Can beginners surf at Wrightsville Beach?

Yes. Wrightsville Beach is a popular place to learn, and local instructors run lessons for first-timers when conditions allow. Check the live surf check on this page before you go.

Surf lessons

When is the best time of year to learn to surf here?

Late spring through early fall, when the water is warm enough to surf without a wetsuit. Mornings usually have the lightest wind.

Live Wilmington weather

Do I need my own surfboard for a lesson?

No. Lessons include a board and start on the sand before you paddle out. Trying a board in a lesson or a rental is also the easiest way to find out what size suits you before you buy one.

Choosing a first surfboard

Is the flag on the surf check an official beach flag?

No. It is a ShoreList estimate worked out from live wave, wind and weather data. The flags posted on the beach and the instructions of lifeguards always come first.

Know before you go

Does ShoreList sell surfboards or kayaks?

No. ShoreList is a local guide and booking marketplace. Our guides explain what to look for, and a local surf shop can fit you in person. The ShoreList shop carries apparel and beach goods, including two surf tees.

ShoreList shop

Do I need a life jacket on a paddleboard or kayak?

Plan on having one for every person. The US Coast Guard treats a paddleboard as a vessel once it is used beyond a swimming, surfing or bathing area, and North Carolina requires anyone under 13 to wear a life jacket on a recreational vessel that is underway. Rules change, so confirm with the official sources linked in our guide.
