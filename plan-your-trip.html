Planning a Trip to Wilmington, NC

When to go, where to stay, how the beaches differ and a three-day plan for Wilmington, Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach.

The short version

Wilmington at a glance

Wilmington sits on the Cape Fear River in southeastern North Carolina, with the Atlantic a few miles east. Visitors get a historic river city and three distinct beach towns in one trip, all within about half an hour of each other.

Season by season

When to go

  • Spring (March to May): mild days, gardens in bloom and the North Carolina Azalea Festival in April. The ocean is still cool.
  • Summer (June to August): peak beach season, warm water and the busiest weekends. Book lodging and tours early, and expect afternoon thunderstorms.
  • Fall (September to November): warm water into October, fewer crowds and good fishing. It is also the heart of hurricane season, so keep plans flexible.
  • Winter (December to February): quiet and mild, with holiday events and lower lodging rates. A good time for downtown, museums and long beach walks.

Itinerary

A simple three-day plan

Day1

Day 1: Downtown and the river

Start with a guided walking tour of the historic district, visit the Wilmington Railroad Museum or cross the river to the Battleship North Carolina, then take a sunset river cruise. After dark, try a ghost tour.

Day2

Day 2: Wrightsville Beach

Check the live beach report, then take a surf lesson or paddleboard the sound side in the morning. In the afternoon, a kayak or wildlife tour gets you out toward Masonboro Island, and a sunset sail ends the day.

Day3

Day 3: Carolina Beach and Kure Beach

Walk the flytrap trail at Carolina Beach State Park on a nature walk, spend midday on the sand and the boardwalk, then continue south to Kure Beach and Fort Fisher. Anglers can swap the morning for a fishing charter.

Getting here and getting around

Wilmington International Airport (ILM) is about 10 minutes from downtown. By road, Interstate 40 ends in Wilmington, and Raleigh is roughly two hours away. Once you are here, downtown is walkable, and each beach town is easy by bike, e-bike or golf cart, but you will want a car to move between them. Beach parking is paid in season, and lots fill early on summer weekends.

What to pack

Sun protection, a hat, sandals and a light layer for evenings on the water. For the beach, bring or rent chairs and shade: you can rent a setup or pick up chairs, umbrellas, towels and coolers from the ShoreList Shop.

Before you go

Trip planning questions

What is the best time of year to visit Wilmington, NC?

Late spring and early fall are the sweet spots: warm weather, swimmable water and thinner crowds. Summer is peak beach season. Spring brings the azaleas and the North Carolina Azalea Festival in April, and winter is quiet and mild, with holiday events downtown.

How many days do you need in Wilmington?

Three days covers the essentials: one for downtown and the riverfront, one for Wrightsville Beach, and one for Carolina Beach, Kure Beach and Fort Fisher. A week lets you slow down and add fishing, paddling and day trips.

Which airport do you fly into for Wilmington, NC?

Wilmington International Airport (ILM) is about 10 minutes from downtown and around 20 to 30 minutes from the beaches. Raleigh-Durham (RDU) is roughly two hours away by car on Interstate 40.

Should I stay downtown or at the beach?

Stay downtown for restaurants, nightlife, history and walkability. Stay at Wrightsville Beach for an upscale beach base close to the city, at Carolina Beach for a classic family beach town with a boardwalk, or at Kure Beach for quiet.

Do you need a car in Wilmington, NC?

Yes, for most trips. Downtown is walkable and the beach towns are easy to get around by bike or golf cart, but you will want a car to move between them.

When is hurricane season on the North Carolina coast?

Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, with the most activity usually in August, September and early October. Trip insurance is worth considering for those months.

Is the ocean warm enough to swim?

Ocean water is warmest from June through September. ShoreList shows the current water temperature, surf and an estimated flag for each beach.
