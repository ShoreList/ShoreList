Getting to Wilson Center at Cape Fear Community College

Wilson Center at Cape Fear Community College is at 703 N Third St, Wilmington, NC 28401. Downtown Wilmington is compact and walkable: the Riverwalk, the historic district, restaurants and shops sit close together.

Parking
The venue's getting-here page lists the Hanover Student Parking Deck at 200 Hanover Street, open 1.5 hours before showtime, at $8 prepaid or $10 cash at the gate. Metered street parking is also available around the Wilson Center. Rates can change, so check the venue page before you go.

Staying nearby

Best for walkability, restaurants, nightlife, history and first-time visitors. Stay downtown and you can walk to the Riverwalk, the historic district, ghost and history tours, river cruises, theaters and most of the city's best-known restaurants. Hotels here range from riverfront high-rises to small inns and historic bed and breakfasts. The beaches are a 20 to 30 minute drive. Compare areas on the where to stay page.
