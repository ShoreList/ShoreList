Events by place

Events cluster in different parts of the area. Downtown Wilmington, along the Riverwalk and the historic district, has the most concerts, theater and history events. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach hold tree lightings, parades, holiday markets and plunges, and Greater Wilmington covers museum exhibitions and campus concerts. Pick a place to see every date there.

  • Downtown Wilmington events: 34 listed. Concerts, theater, markets, parades and history events along the Riverwalk and in the historic district.
  • Wrightsville Beach events: 4 listed. Tree lightings, the holiday flotilla, races, plunges and community events on the island.
  • Carolina Beach events: 5 listed. Boardwalk and lake events, parades, markets, movies and seasonal celebrations.
  • Kure Beach events: 4 listed. Markets, holiday gatherings, plunges and events near the pier and Fort Fisher.
  • Greater Wilmington events: 11 listed. Museum exhibitions, garden light shows, campus concerts and races across the wider city.
