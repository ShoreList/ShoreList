About Kure Beach

Best for quiet stays, the fishing pier, history and nature. Kure Beach sits just past Carolina Beach on the same island. It is the quietest of the three beach towns, with a fishing pier, a small oceanfront park, and Fort Fisher State Historic Site and its recreation area nearby. Lodging is mostly cottages, small motels and vacation rentals.

Markets, holiday gatherings, plunges and events near the pier and Fort Fisher. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach each set their own paid parking seasons, hours and rates, usually paid by app or kiosk, so check the town website and arrive early on busy days. See the Kure Beach guide for things to do and the where to stay page to compare areas.
