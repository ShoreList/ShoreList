About Downtown Wilmington

Best for walkability, restaurants, nightlife, history and first-time visitors. Stay downtown and you can walk to the Riverwalk, the historic district, ghost and history tours, river cruises, theaters and most of the city's best-known restaurants. Hotels here range from riverfront high-rises to small inns and historic bed and breakfasts. The beaches are a 20 to 30 minute drive.

Concerts, theater, markets, parades and history events along the Riverwalk and in the historic district. See the Downtown Wilmington guide for things to do and the where to stay page to compare areas.
