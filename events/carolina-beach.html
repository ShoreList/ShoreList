About Carolina Beach

Best for families, the boardwalk, fishing and a classic beach-town feel. Carolina Beach is about 30 minutes south of downtown on Pleasure Island. It has a boardwalk with rides and food in season, a lake park, a state park with trails and a marina, and plenty of fishing charters. You will find oceanfront hotels, motels, condos and a large supply of vacation rentals.

Boardwalk and lake events, parades, markets, movies and seasonal celebrations. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach each set their own paid parking seasons, hours and rates, usually paid by app or kiosk, so check the town website and arrive early on busy days. See the Carolina Beach guide for things to do and the where to stay page to compare areas.
