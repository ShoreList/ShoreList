Carolina Beach, NC
Wild, Warm, and Real.

A classic American beach town with a boardwalk that's been rocking since 1939, the only wild Venus flytraps in the world, and the best July 4th fireworks on the East Coast. Carolina Beach doesn't pretend to be anything it isn't — and that's exactly why people love it.

Venus Flytrap Nature Walk
Wild Venus flytraps grow in only one place in the world — a narrow band of the NC coastal plain, right here. A guided walk through Carolina Beach State Park takes you into ancient cypress swamps and the carnivorous plant bogs that make this state park unlike anywhere else on Earth.
