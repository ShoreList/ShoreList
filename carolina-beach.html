Quick answers

Carolina Beach: quick answers

Carolina Beach is the busiest town on Pleasure Island, about 30 minutes south of downtown Wilmington and about 19 miles from Wilmington International Airport. It has a boardwalk, a charter boat basin and Carolina Beach State Park, and the visitors bureau says there is no charge to access the beach.

Is the beach free at Carolina Beach?

Access to the beach has no charge. Parking is separate: in 2026 paid parking ran March 1 to October 31, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., and was free November 1 to the end of February. See the parking guide and confirm with the town.

Are dogs allowed on the beach at Carolina Beach?

Dogs must be leashed. From April 1 to September 30 they are allowed on the beach only before 9 a.m. and after 5 p.m., and from October 1 to March 31 at any hour. They are not allowed on the historic wooden boardwalk or in the Boardwalk Business District. See the town dog rules.

Can you drive on the beach at Carolina Beach?

Only four-wheel-drive vehicles, and only at Freeman Park, which requires a paid permit and also allows overnight camping. Read the town Freeman Park rules first.

Where can I see Venus flytraps near Carolina Beach?

On the Flytrap Trail and elsewhere in Carolina Beach State Park, according to the visitors bureau. Most day-use activities at the park, such as hiking, biking and fishing, have no fee. See nature walks near Wilmington.
