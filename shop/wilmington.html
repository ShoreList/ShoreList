Questions

Where do the beach towel and tote ship from?

Everything is made to order by our print partners. Shipping method, cost and the estimate for your address are shown at checkout.

Is everything made to order?

Yes. Every piece is made after you order, so allow production time before it ships. The shipping and returns page has the details.

How does checkout work?

Pieces from ShoreList Shop go in your bag and check out together in one bag. One piece, the organic cotton market tote, checks out on its own page. Shipping and tax are shown before you pay.

What if something arrives wrong?

If a piece arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or in the wrong size, we replace or refund it at no cost when you report it within 30 days of delivery. Made-to-order pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind or size preference.
