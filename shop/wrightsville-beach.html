Wrightsville Beach

Wrightsville Beach souvenirs, made to order

The ShoreList Shop sells Wrightsville Beach NC souvenirs and gifts, each with one small, understated mark: the sloop, for sailing days and marina evenings. A sloop pattern tote, mug, pillow, pouch, tea towel and pet bandana, embroidered crewnecks and caps, and a can cooler, all made to order.

Before you shop the beach

Wrightsville Beach is about 20 minutes east of downtown Wilmington. Beach access has no charge. Parking meters run from March 1 to October 31, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., and some meters until 8 p.m. Peak surf season on the island is June to September, including the end of Johnnie Mercer's Pier. The full guide is on the Wrightsville Beach page, with the parking guide, Johnnie Mercer's Pier and surf lessons.

These notes are the same facts already on that guide, checked against the visitors bureau and the town. Rules and fees change, so confirm before you go.

Questions

What is the Wrightsville Beach mark?

A small sloop under sail. On apparel it is stitched small, and on home and beach goods it repeats as a navy pattern.

Is everything made to order?

Yes. Every piece is made after you order, so allow production time before it ships. The shipping and returns page has the details.

How does checkout work?

Pieces from ShoreList Shop go in your bag and check out together in one bag. One piece, the organic cotton market tote, checks out on its own page. Shipping and tax are shown before you pay.

What if something arrives wrong?

If a piece arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or in the wrong size, we replace or refund it at no cost when you report it within 30 days of delivery. Made-to-order pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind or size preference.
