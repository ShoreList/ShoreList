Questions

What is the Wrightsville Beach mark?

A small sloop under sail. On apparel it is stitched on the chest, the cap or the beanie cuff.

Is everything made to order?

Yes. Every piece is made after you order, so allow production time before it ships. The shipping and returns page has the details.

How does checkout work?

Pieces from ShoreList Shop go in your bag and check out together on Shopify. A few pieces are sold through our second store, ShoreList Gear, and check out there. Shipping and tax are shown before you pay.

What if something arrives wrong?

If a piece arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or in the wrong size, we replace it at no cost when you report it within 30 days of delivery. Made-to-order pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind or size preference.
