Where to stay in Wilmington, NC

Downtown Wilmington and the riverfront

Best for walkability, restaurants, nightlife, history and first-time visitors.

Stay downtown and you can walk to the Riverwalk, the historic district, ghost and history tours, river cruises, theaters and most of the city's best-known restaurants. Hotels here range from riverfront high-rises to small inns and historic bed and breakfasts. The beaches are a 20 to 30 minute drive.

What you will find: Riverfront hotels such as Hotel Ballast and Embassy Suites by Hilton Wilmington Riverfront, plus boutique inns and bed and breakfasts in the historic district.

Wrightsville Beach

Best for surfing, paddleboarding, clear water and the closest beach to the city.

Wrightsville Beach is about 20 minutes from downtown. It is a small island with a walkable beach strand, the Loop walking path, water sports on the sound side and a relaxed, active feel. Lodging is limited, so it books early for summer and holiday weekends. Paid parking applies in season.

What you will find: Oceanfront resorts such as Blockade Runner Beach Resort and Holiday Inn Resort Lumina on Wrightsville Beach, plus vacation rentals.

Carolina Beach

Best for families, the boardwalk, fishing and a classic beach-town feel.

Carolina Beach is about 30 minutes south of downtown on Pleasure Island. It has a boardwalk with rides and food in season, a lake park, a state park with trails and a marina, and plenty of fishing charters. You will find oceanfront hotels, motels, condos and a large supply of vacation rentals.

What you will find: Oceanfront hotels such as Courtyard by Marriott Carolina Beach Oceanfront and Golden Sands, plus condos and beach house rentals.

Carolina Beach guide Carolina Beach events Fishing charters

Kure Beach and Fort Fisher

Best for quiet stays, the fishing pier, history and nature.

Kure Beach sits just past Carolina Beach on the same island. It is the quietest of the three beach towns, with a fishing pier, a small oceanfront park, and Fort Fisher State Historic Site and its recreation area nearby. Lodging is mostly cottages, small motels and vacation rentals.

What you will find: Beach cottages, small family-run motels and vacation rentals.

Kure Beach guide Kure Beach events Nature walks

Midtown, Mayfaire and near the airport

Best for value, road trips, shopping and easy parking.

Chain hotels are spread along the main roads between downtown and Wrightsville Beach, around the Mayfaire shopping area, near UNCW and close to Wilmington International Airport (ILM). You will drive everywhere, but rates are often lower than on the water and parking is usually simple.

What you will find: Familiar national hotel brands and extended-stay properties.

Where to stay: common questions

What is the best area to stay in Wilmington, NC?

For a first visit, downtown Wilmington is the easiest base: it is walkable, close to restaurants and tours, and 20 to 30 minutes from the beaches. If the beach is the whole point, stay at Wrightsville Beach for surfing and water sports, Carolina Beach for families and the boardwalk, or Kure Beach for a quiet stay.

Is it better to stay downtown or at the beach?

Stay downtown if you want restaurants, nightlife, history and tours on foot. Stay at the beach if you plan to spend most days on the sand. Many visitors split a trip, or stay downtown and drive to a different beach each day.

How far are the beach hotels from downtown Wilmington?

Wrightsville Beach is about 20 minutes from downtown. Carolina Beach is about 30 minutes, and Kure Beach is a few minutes beyond it. Allow extra time on summer weekends.

When should I book a place to stay in Wilmington?

Summer, holiday weekends and major festival weekends fill first, especially on the beaches where lodging is limited. Book early for those dates. Late fall through early spring usually has more availability.

Are there vacation rentals in Wilmington and the beach towns?

Yes. Beach houses, condos and cottages are common in Carolina Beach, Kure Beach and Wrightsville Beach, and there are rentals downtown as well. Check each listing for minimum stays, parking and local rules.

Does ShoreList book hotels?

No. ShoreList books local tours and experiences and helps you plan what to do. Book lodging directly with the hotel or rental host. The hotels named on this page are examples, not rankings or endorsements.
