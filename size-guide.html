Size guide

Fit, measured

Every piece is made to order, so the right size matters. Here are the real numbers where we have them, and a straight answer where we do not.

How to measure

The most reliable method is also the least glamorous. Lay a crewneck or sweatshirt you already like flat on a bed, and measure it the way the chart does.

  • Chest, flat. Straight across the chest, one inch below the underarm seam.
  • Length. From the high point of the shoulder, beside the collar, down the back to the hem.

Match those numbers to the chart. If you land between two sizes, choose by the garment you love, not by the size on its tag.

Embroidered crewnecks

These are garment measurements: the piece laid flat, not your body. Chest, around is simply the flat width doubled, so you can compare it to a tape measure worn around the chest with room to move.

Allow about an inch either way. The pieces are garment-dyed, which means they are dyed after they are sewn, so color and size both move a little from one piece to the next.

This chart is for the four embroidered crewnecks. If a product page shows its own size information, that page wins.

Everything else

Corduroy caps, cuffed beanies, printed tees, totes and beach gear do not have a chart here yet. We would rather leave a gap than print a number we could not source to the maker. Sizing for each piece is shown on its product page.

If it is not there, or you are deciding between two sizes, write to us with the piece and your usual size, and we will answer with what we can confirm.

Made to order, so ask first

Every piece is made after you order. A size swap after the fact is not something we can promise, which is why a fit question beforehand is welcome. The shipping and returns page explains what we can do when something arrives wrong.
