ShoreList LLC — Operator Listing Agreement
Plain English · Fair Terms

1. Platform Services

ShoreList LLC agrees to list your business on Shorelist.net, market your experiences to visitors through our website and physical promotional materials, process guest booking deposits on your behalf, notify you of all new bookings by email, and remit your net payout within 5 business days of a completed experience.

2. Your Obligations as an Operator

  • Respond to booking notifications within 24 hours.
  • Maintain all required licenses, permits, and certifications to legally operate your business.
  • Carry commercial general liability insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, naming ShoreList LLC as an additional insured.
  • Provide accurate, honest descriptions of your experiences including pricing, duration, and safety requirements.
  • Deliver confirmed bookings as described.
  • Notify ShoreList within 48 hours of any pricing or availability changes.
  • Treat all ShoreList-referred guests professionally and courteously.
  • Not solicit ShoreList-referred guests to bypass our platform during this agreement and for 12 months after termination.

3. Commission & Payment

ShoreList earns a commission on every completed booking. There are no listing fees, monthly fees, or setup charges. ShoreList earns only when you earn. Payouts are processed within 5 business days of the completed experience.

4. Refunds & Cancellations

If you cancel: Guests receive a full refund. You receive no payment for cancelled bookings. If a guest cancels: ShoreList's standard cancellation policy applies. No-shows: You retain the booking deposit for confirmed no-shows.

5. Representations & Warranties

By signing, you confirm that you have legal authority to enter this agreement, all information you provide is accurate, your experiences comply with all applicable laws, and you will maintain all required operating licenses.

6. Intellectual Property

You grant ShoreList a limited, non-exclusive license to use your business name, photos, and descriptions solely to promote your listings on our platform. You retain full ownership of all your content.

7. Liability & Indemnification

ShoreList is a booking marketplace and does not operate or supervise experiences. You agree to indemnify ShoreList LLC against third-party claims arising from your business operations. ShoreList's total liability to you is limited to 3 months of commissions paid in the preceding period.

8. Term & Termination

This agreement runs month-to-month from the date of signing. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. ShoreList may terminate immediately for cause including violation of any applicable law or repeated failure to honor confirmed bookings.

9. Tax Reporting — IRS Form 1099-K & W-9

ShoreList is required by federal law to report payments to operators. If you receive $600 or more in gross payments through ShoreList in any calendar year, ShoreList will issue you an IRS Form 1099-K. You are solely responsible for reporting your ShoreList income on your own tax returns.

10. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina. You and ShoreList are independent contractors. Any unresolved disputes are subject to good-faith mediation in New Hanover County, NC before litigation.
