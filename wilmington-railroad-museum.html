Wilmington Railroad Museum

For over a century, Wilmington was headquarters of the Atlantic Coast Line Railroad — and that story lives on inside an authentic 1883 freight warehouse at the north end of downtown. Climb aboard a real steam locomotive, walk through a life-size caboose, and lose track of time in one of the largest model train layouts on the coast. Fun for every age, rain or shine.

Inside the Museum

What You’ll Experience

🚂
Climb Aboard a 1911 Baldwin Steam Locomotive
Step into the cab of a real steam engine, ring the bell, and stand where Wilmington’s railroaders stood. Outside the museum entrance, the old locomotive is one of downtown’s most photographed landmarks.
Hands-OnPhoto Favorite
🚆
Walk Through a Life-Size Caboose
Explore an authentic caboose and see how crews lived and worked on the line — part of a collection of artifacts, photographs, and exhibits tracing the Atlantic Coast Line era and the railroad’s role in building Wilmington.
Real Rolling StockACL History
🗺️
20+ Running Model Trains
The grand finale: a 1,600-square-foot model railroad with more than twenty trains running through miniature towns and coastal scenery — with plenty of buttons for kids (and adults) to press.
1,600 Sq Ft LayoutInteractive
🧩
Scavenger Hunts & Children’s Play Area
In-museum scavenger hunts, hands-on displays, a small theater with railroad films, and a sensory-safe wooden train play area make this an easy win for families — most visits run about an hour.

Getting There

📍 Location

505 Nutt Street, Wilmington, NC 28401 — at the north end of downtown, an easy walk from the Riverwalk and the Cotton Exchange. (910) 763-2634.

🕑 Hours

Summer (Memorial Day–Labor Day): Mon–Sat 10–5, Sun 1–5. Winter: Mon–Sat 10–4, closed Sundays. Arrive 45 minutes before close to see everything.

🚗 Parking & Trolley

Limited free parking in designated spots in front of and behind the old locomotive, plus nearby garages and metered parking. The free downtown trolley stops right in front of the museum.
