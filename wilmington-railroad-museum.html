Wilmington Railroad Museum

For more than a century, Wilmington was the headquarters of the Atlantic Coast Line Railroad — and this museum, housed in an authentic 1883 freight warehouse at 505 Nutt Street, keeps that story running. Climb aboard a 1911 Baldwin steam locomotive, explore a life-size caboose, and watch 20+ model trains roll through a 1,600 sq ft layout.

501(c)(3) Nonprofit Since 1979 All Ages & Abilities Indoor — Rain or Shine

📍 Plan Your Visit

Getting here

Location

505 Nutt Street, Wilmington, NC 28401 — the north end of downtown, an easy walk from the Riverwalk and Coastline Center. Phone: 910-763-2634.

Parking & Trolley

Limited free museum parking in the designated spots in front of and behind the old locomotive, plus nearby garages and metered street parking. The free downtown trolley stops right in front of the museum.
