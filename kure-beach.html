NC Aquarium at Fort Fisher — Closed for $65 Million Expansion
The main aquarium at 900 Loggerhead Road closed May 26, 2026 for a major renovation estimated to take approximately 30 months. When complete, it will be the largest aquarium in North Carolina. During construction, the aquarium team has opened Discovery Cove — a satellite location at Independence Mall in Wilmington with animal encounters, marine life habitats, and education programs. Coastal Carolina Nature Walks
The Fort Fisher and Kure Beach area sits at one of the most biologically rich points on the East Coast. Guided walks through maritime forest, coastal dunes, and tidal wetlands reveal an extraordinary diversity of species. Wild Venus flytraps at nearby Carolina Beach State Park are included on select tours. Led by ShoreList founder RJ Brideau.

Fort Fisher State Historic Site
Fort Fisher was the last major Confederate fortification to fall in the Civil War, defending the Cape Fear River’s supply route until January 1865. The site contains original earthworks, a museum, and sweeping Atlantic views where the Union fleet assembled for the war’s largest combined naval and land attack.

Fort Fisher State Recreation Area
Four miles of wild, undeveloped Atlantic beach at the southern tip of Pleasure Island. Protected nesting habitat for loggerhead sea turtles and imperiled shorebirds. No development, no crowds — just the Atlantic the way it looked a hundred years ago.

NC Aquarium at Fort Fisher
900 Loggerhead Road, Kure Beach  ·  Closed for renovation & expansion
Under Construction
The Fort Fisher Aquarium closed May 26, 2026 for a $65 million renovation and expansion expected to take approximately 30 months. During this time, visit Discovery Cove at Independence Mall in Wilmington — the aquarium’s satellite location featuring animal encounters, marine life habitats, and education programs. Free admission. ncaquariums.com ↗
When it reopens, the NC Aquarium at Fort Fisher will be the largest aquarium in North Carolina — featuring a 400,000-gallon sand tiger shark habitat, a Pacific live coral reef, an interactive shark and ray touch pool, a state-of-the-art education center, and a rooftop sky deck overlooking the Atlantic. Its first major renovation in 20 years. Worth coming back for.
