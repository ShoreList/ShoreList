Fort Fisher State Historic Site

Fort Fisher was the last major Confederate fortification to fall in the Civil War, defending the Cape Fear River’s supply route until January 1865. The site contains original earthworks, a museum, and sweeping Atlantic views where the Union fleet assembled for a combined naval and land attack.

Fort Fisher State Recreation Area

Four miles of wild, undeveloped Atlantic beach at the southern tip of Pleasure Island. Protected nesting habitat for loggerhead sea turtles and imperiled shorebirds. No development, no crowds. Just the Atlantic the way it looked a hundred years ago.

Quick answers

Kure Beach: quick answers

Kure Beach, pronounced "Cure-ee", is a town on Pleasure Island between Carolina Beach and Fort Fisher, with wide sandy beaches and a historic fishing pier. The North Carolina Aquarium at Fort Fisher is closed for construction, but Fort Fisher State Historic Site has free general admission and the state recreation area is open.

How do you pronounce Kure Beach?

"Cure-ee," according to the Town of Kure Beach.

Is the North Carolina Aquarium at Fort Fisher open?

Not for now. It closed to the public on May 26, 2026 for construction that the state expects to take 30 months, and no reopening date has been given. See the state announcement.

Is Fort Fisher State Historic Site free?

General admission is free. The site is open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Sunday, Monday and designated state holidays. Guided tours cost extra. Details are on the NC Historic Sites page.

Can you drive on the beach at Fort Fisher?

At Fort Fisher State Recreation Area, yes, with a four-wheel-drive vehicle during posted hours and a per-vehicle fee. NC State Parks says it is the only park in its system that allows four-wheel-drive beach access. See the park page.

When is paid parking at Kure Beach?

In 2026 it ran March 15 to September 30, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., per local news. Each town sets its dates yearly, so see the parking guide and confirm with the town.
