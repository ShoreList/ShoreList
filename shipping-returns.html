Shipping and returns

Shipping & returns, plainly

Made to order, shipped by our partners, fixed if it arrives wrong. Here is exactly how that works.

  • Made after you order.Nothing is printed or stitched until you buy. That is why we cannot restock a returned piece, and why we say so up front.
  • One shop, one bag.Everything on the shop page goes in one bag and checks out together. The organic cotton market tote is the one exception and checks out on its own page.
  • The cost, before you pay.Shipping and tax are calculated at checkout and shown before you confirm.
  • Problems get fixed.If a piece arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, write to us with photos and we will replace or refund it.

One shop

The embroidered and printed apparel, the beach gear, the can coolers, the ornaments and the framed prints are all in the ShoreList shop and check out together in one bag. The organic cotton market tote is the one piece that opens on its own page and checks out there. Order it with other pieces and you get two orders, which may arrive on different days.

How long it takes

Production. Production begins after checkout. Our print partner states that most orders ship within 2 to 7 business days of being submitted, and the exact time depends on the season, the product and the order size. We do not quote a faster number for any single piece.

Delivery. Transit time depends on where you are. The shipping method, cost and estimated delivery for your address are shown at checkout, before you pay.

If it goes quiet. If your order shows no update after 7 business days, write to us and we will find out why.

Gifts. We do not publish holiday order-by dates, because we cannot source one from our partners. The estimate at checkout is the one to trust.

Changes, split orders and addresses

  • The shipping method and timing cannot be changed after the order is placed, and an order with several items may arrive in more than one parcel.
  • Check your address before you pay. Our print partners do not replace packages lost because of an incorrect address.

Returns and replacements

Damaged, defective or misprinted

Write to rj@shorelist.net as soon as you notice, with your order number and photos of the problem. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost. Please report it within 30 days of delivery. Wrong size or wrong item sent also counts. Misprints, faded or smeared designs and damage that affected the piece all count.

If your order has not arrived 30 days after it was placed, write to us within one week after that point and we will investigate.

Some things are not defects. Garment-dyed pieces vary slightly in color from one to the next, ink on dark fabric can read softer than it does on a screen, and a measurement within about 1 inch of the size guide is within tolerance.

Change of mind and size swaps

Because every piece is made after you order, we do not accept returns for a change of mind or a size preference. A size exchange applies only if the wrong size was sent. Customers in the EU, EEA or UK may have statutory withdrawal rights, which this policy does not limit. A question about fit costs nothing, and the size guide is the better first step.

Guides and prints

The PDF guides and art print files are downloads, and nothing ships. Seven percent North Carolina sales tax is added at checkout. If a file is missing or will not open, email rj@shorelist.net.
