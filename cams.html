Live beach cams in Wilmington, NC

This is the live-cam desk for Wilmington and the island beaches. Two players on this page are the official Johnnie Mercer's Pier YouTube cams at Wrightsville Beach, south view and north view. A third slot is the Southport Marina channel, which plays only when that channel is actually live. Every other camera is a link that opens on the owner's site. ShoreList does not operate any of these cameras, and it does not restream them.

What plays here, and what does not

The south and north pier cams are published by Johnnie Mercer's Fishing Pier on its own YouTube channel, and the pier links those views from its live cam page. ShoreList uses that official player, muted until you unmute it inside the player. Press play on one tile. On a phone, starting a second cam closes the first, so the page does not run a stack of video frames. Nothing starts until you press play.

SurfChex runs the wide local network: Wrightsville Beach, the Intracoastal at the drawbridge, downtown Wilmington, Carolina Beach, the Kure Beach Fishing Pier, Topsail and Surf City, Southport and Oak Island. Those pages stay links. There is no published license for ShoreList to play them, so they are not embedded. Surfline's Wrightsville Beach reports, DriveNC bridge cameras, the WRAL river cam, and the WebCOOS camera at Masonboro Inlet are the same kind of link. Masonboro Island itself does not have a beach-front player on this page. The inlet camera is a conditions camera on the WebCOOS site.

Bald Head Island Beach Access 15 can play in the Southport and Oak Island filter. It is a nearby view, credited to Steve Montgomery of Coast Homes NC, and it is not one of the three island beaches in the Wrightsville, Carolina and Kure set. The Frying Pan Tower cams are offshore, about 34 miles seaward of Cape Fear by the owner's own description, and they are only a link.

Tide height on the pier tiles comes from NOAA station 8658163 at Wrightsville Beach, the same station the rest of ShoreList uses. Wind comes from Open-Meteo. Carolina Beach and Kure Beach tiles use those towns' existing ShoreList weather points for wind, and they still label the tide as the Wrightsville Beach station because that is the station we actually read. Topsail, Southport, Oak Island and the river tiles say so when the numbers are the Wrightsville Beach reading. If the feed has not arrived, the chip stays blank. A colored flag anywhere on ShoreList is an estimate, not an official lifeguard flag.
