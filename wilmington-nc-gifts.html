People usually want a Wilmington NC gift for a host on Front Street, a neighbor, or a visitor who walked the Wilmington Riverwalk and still has room in a suitcase. The useful ones name the city or the beach a short drive east. Airlie Gardens is a public garden at 300 Airlie Road in Wilmington, a few minutes northeast of downtown and close to Wrightsville Beach. The Airlie Oak ornament and mug on this page show an illustration of that oak. ShoreList is an independent company and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Airlie Gardens. Visit details are on the garden's own site.

Wrightsville Beach is the strand most of these gifts point to. Banks Channel is the sheltered water on the sound side. Johnnie Mercer's Pier is the fishing pier on the ocean side. The pier's own site describes it as the first concrete fishing pier in North Carolina, in the heart of Wrightsville Beach, extending more than 1,200 feet into the Atlantic. ShoreList is not affiliated with the pier and does not use its logo. Hours and rules are on the pier's own site. South of the strand, Masonboro Inlet lies between Wrightsville Beach and Masonboro Island. Masonboro Island is undeveloped and reached by boat, not by a road from the beach.
