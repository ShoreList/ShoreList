Carolina Beach

The Carolina Beach boardwalk

Notes for the boardwalk at Carolina Beach, on Pleasure Island. These are the same facts already on the Carolina Beach and Pleasure Island guides, with their sources. This page does not add events, prices or hours of its own.

Carolina Beach is the busiest town on Pleasure Island, about 30 minutes south of downtown Wilmington and about 19 miles from Wilmington International Airport, according to the visitors bureau. The town has a boardwalk, a charter boat basin and Carolina Beach State Park. Access to the beach has no charge. Parking is separate.

What the sources say

  • Beach access. No charge. The parking guide records the 2026 paid parking season as March 1 to October 31, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., and free from November 1 to the end of February. Confirm with the town before you go.
  • Dogs. Not allowed on the historic wooden boardwalk or in the Boardwalk Business District. On the beach they must be leashed. From April 1 to September 30 they are allowed only before 9 a.m. and after 5 p.m., and from October 1 to March 31 at any hour. The rule is on the town pet page.
  • Fireworks. The visitors bureau says weekly fireworks run on Thursday evenings from Memorial Day through Labor Day. For the next dated listing, use the Carolina Beach events page. This page does not invent a show.
  • A live look. The Carolina Beach guide links a boardwalk camera.
  • Walks. ShoreList lists walking tours that cover mural and boardwalk history on Pleasure Island. Bookable tours and their prices are on that page.
