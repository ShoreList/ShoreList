Wilmington weather by season

Spring

Mild and getting warmer, with azaleas in bloom and comfortable days for walking tours, kayaking and the Riverwalk.

Summer

Hot and humid with warm ocean water. Afternoon thunderstorms are common, so plan beach time early and keep an indoor backup.

Fall

Warm water lingers and crowds thin out. It is also the peak of hurricane season, so watch the forecast when you book.

Winter

Cool and quiet, with mild sunny days mixed with cold snaps. A good season for museums, ghost walks, restaurants and holiday events.

Weather questions

What is the weather in Wilmington, NC right now?

The top of this page shows the current temperature, feels-like temperature, wind, humidity and rain for Wilmington, and you can switch to Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach or Kure Beach. It refreshes every 10 minutes.

When is the best time to visit Wilmington and Wrightsville Beach?

Late spring and early fall usually bring warm days, lower humidity and fewer crowds. Summer is the classic beach season, with hot, humid afternoons and the warmest ocean water.

When is hurricane season in Wilmington, NC?

Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, and activity typically peaks from mid-August through October. Check the National Weather Service and National Hurricane Center before and during a trip in those months.

How warm is the ocean at Wrightsville Beach?

The water is usually warmest in late summer and coolest in mid-winter. The live beach report on this page shows the water temperature measured at the NOAA station at Wrightsville Beach.

Is this an official forecast?

No. ShoreList shows forecast data from Open-Meteo and tide and water data from NOAA. For official forecasts, watches and warnings, use the National Weather Service office in Wilmington, NC. The beach flag shown here is an estimate, so always follow the flags and lifeguards on the beach.
