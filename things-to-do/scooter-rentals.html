Scooter Rentals near Wilmington, NC: what to know

Scooters are a simple way to get around a beach town: easy to park, quick for short hops between the beach, the boardwalk and dinner.

Where: Carolina Beach, Kure Beach, Wrightsville Beach and downtown Wilmington.

Before you go

Common questions

Do I need a license to rent a scooter?

It depends on the type of scooter and its engine size. Rental shops will tell you the license and age requirements for each model.

Are scooters allowed on beach-town roads?

Street-legal scooters are allowed on local roads. Follow posted speed limits and wear a helmet.

Is parking free at the beach?

Often, yes. Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach each set their own paid parking seasons, hours and rates, usually paid by app or kiosk. Check the town website before you go and arrive early on summer weekends. A scooter can be easier to park than a car, but the same rules and meters may apply, so ask the rental shop.
