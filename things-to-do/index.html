Local guide

Things to do in Wilmington, NC and the beaches

Wilmington is a river city with three ocean beaches within a half hour: Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach and Kure Beach. That mix is what makes it easy to plan. You can spend the morning on the sand, the afternoon in a 250-year-old historic district, and the evening on the Cape Fear River.

ShoreList is the local guide and booking marketplace for the area. Every category above has its own page with what to expect, where it happens and whether it can be booked on ShoreList yet, and the live beach report on each beach page shows surf, tide, water temperature and an estimated flag.

By type of trip

  • On the water: surf lessons, paddleboarding, kayak tours, fishing charters, sunset sails, river cruises and jet skis.
  • History and stories: walking tours, ghost tours, film and TV location tours and museums.
  • Nature: dolphin and wildlife tours and nature walks, including the wild Venus flytraps at Carolina Beach State Park.
  • Getting around: golf carts, e-bikes, bike tours, fat bikes and scooters.
  • Beach day extras: chair and umbrella rentals, beach picnics, beach photographers and yoga and wellness.

Before you go

Common questions

What are the best things to do in Wilmington, NC?

Walk the Riverwalk and the historic district downtown, tour the Battleship North Carolina, take a river cruise at sunset, and spend a day at Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach or Kure Beach. Ghost tours, film location tours, surf lessons, kayak trips and fishing charters are common guided experiences.

How far are the beaches from downtown Wilmington?

Wrightsville Beach is about 20 minutes east of downtown. Carolina Beach is about 30 minutes south, and Kure Beach is just beyond it on the same island. Allow extra time on summer weekends.

What is there to do in Wilmington when it rains?

Museums such as the Wilmington Railroad Museum, historic house tours, film location tours, and spa and wellness experiences all work in bad weather.

Which beach near Wilmington should I choose?

Wrightsville Beach is closest and popular for surfing and paddleboarding. Carolina Beach has the boardwalk, a state park and a lively family atmosphere. Kure Beach is the quietest, with a fishing pier and Fort Fisher nearby.
