Ghost Tours near Wilmington, NC: what to know

Wilmington is an old port city, and its historic district is full of the kind of history that makes a good ghost story: old cemeteries, antebellum homes, brick alleys and a working riverfront that dates to the 1700s. Ghost tours here are walking tours after dark, usually through the blocks between the Cape Fear River and Third Street.

Where: Downtown Wilmington historic district and the Riverwalk.

Local places to know

Wilmington Riverwalk

The Riverwalk runs 1.75 miles along the Cape Fear River, from the foot of Nun Street to the Isabel Holmes Bridge. Evening tours stay in the historic district a few blocks away, and the river is the natural place for a walk before or after.

Burgwin-Wright House and Gardens

This historic house museum stands at 224 Market Street, inside the district where most ghost tours walk. Visit in daylight to see the house itself, then come back after dark for the stories.

Battleship North Carolina

The World War II battleship sits across the river from downtown at 1 Battleship Road and is open for self-guided tours. It pairs well with a ghost tour as a daytime stop on the same trip.

Book the evening tour first, then fill the day around it. Downtown has five city parking decks, so you can park once and walk to the tour meeting point, the river and dinner.

Before you go

Common questions

Where do Wilmington ghost tours take place?

Almost all of them are walking tours in the downtown historic district, within a few blocks of the Cape Fear River. Wear comfortable shoes, since the streets are brick and uneven in places.

Are ghost tours good for kids?

Most are story-driven walking tours rather than jump-scare attractions, so older kids usually enjoy them. Check the age guidance on each listing before you book.
