Museums & Attractions near Wilmington, NC: what to know

Wilmington packs a lot of museums into a small downtown: railroad history, a World War II battleship moored across the river, historic house museums and gardens. They make a good plan for a rainy day or a break from the beach, and most are within a short walk or drive of the Riverwalk.

Where: Downtown Wilmington, the riverfront and Fort Fisher near Kure Beach.

Local places to know

Battleship North Carolina

The World War II battleship is moored at 1 Battleship Road and is open for self-guided tours. Plan on a good part of a morning, and check the ticket site for current hours.

Cameron Art Museum

Cameron Art Museum at 3201 South 17th Street is an art museum and a calm indoor choice when the weather turns.

Children's Museum of Wilmington

The Children's Museum is at 116 Orange Street, downtown, and is built for younger kids who need to touch and play.

Fort Fisher State Historic Site

South of Kure Beach, general admission to the Fort Fisher State Historic Site is free and donations are welcome. It is open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Sunday, Monday and designated state holidays.

Mix one downtown museum with one on the south end of the island and you have two easy days without much driving. Always check each museum's own page for hours before you go.

Before you go

Common questions

What is there to do in Wilmington, NC on a rainy day?

Museums are a good option. The Wilmington Railroad Museum downtown is indoors and family friendly, and the historic house museums run guided tours in any weather.

Are Wilmington museums good for young children?

Yes. The Railroad Museum in particular is built around hands-on exhibits, model trains and real rail cars that kids can climb aboard.
