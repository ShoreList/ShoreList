Wrightsville Beach is about 20 minutes east of downtown Wilmington. At Wrightsville Beach, beach access has no charge and parking meters run from March 1 to October 31, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., and some meters until 8 p.m. Peak surf season on the island is June to September.

The full notes are on the [Wrightsville Beach guide](/wrightsville-beach), the [parking guide](/wilmington-nc-parking) and [Johnnie Mercer's Pier](/johnnie-mercers-pier). Rules and fees change, so confirm before you go.
