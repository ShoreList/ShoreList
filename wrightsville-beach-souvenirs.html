Wrightsville Beach souvenirs to take home

Wrightsville Beach souvenirs on this page name the beach in a small way: a sloop, the letters WB, the town name on a can cooler, or the year on an ornament. Gifts under $35 come first. The embroidered crewneck is the larger piece.

A Wrightsville Beach souvenir works when the person who gets it can tell where it is from without a long explanation. The can cooler and the can cooler sleeve are the beach-day pieces. The ornament is the dated keepsake. Mugs, beanies, zip pouches, the tea towel and the pet bandana cover the house, the head and the dog. The sea oats sticker is the smallest of the set. None of these is a town seal or a photo of a landmark.

On the ocean side, Johnnie Mercer's Pier is the fishing pier in the heart of Wrightsville Beach. Its own site says it was the first concrete fishing pier in North Carolina and that it extends more than 1,200 feet into the Atlantic. That is a landmark, not a partnership. ShoreList does not use the pier's logo. Check hours on the pier's own site. Banks Channel is the calmer water on the sound side. The Loop at Wrightsville Beach crosses Banks Channel and passes Wrightsville Beach Park, an easy walk if the gift is for someone who already spends mornings there. Masonboro Inlet is the inlet south of the strand, between Wrightsville Beach and Masonboro Island. The island is undeveloped and reached by boat.
