How the gift list works

The finder is for people buying from the ShoreList shop. Set a ceiling of $25, $35 or $50. Then choose a list: for him, for her, or for the home. You can also type a plain request, such as a boat gift or a kitchen gift. Wrightsville Beach leads every list, including pieces for Banks Channel and days at Johnnie Mercer's Pier. Carolina Beach, Kure Beach and Wilmington follow.

For him leans toward can coolers, caps, beanies, tees and stickers. For her leans toward pouches, ornaments, mugs and totes. For the home leans toward mugs, towels, pillows, ornaments and prints. Those are suggestions, not departments. Every piece is unisex unless its own product page says otherwise.

Questions

Where do Gift Finder prices come from?

Each price is read from the live ShoreList shop when that shop answers. If it does not answer, the page keeps the last catalog price and says so. Shipping and tax are added at checkout.

Can Wrighty suggest a product that is not in the shop?

No. Wrighty can only return handles that are in the ShoreList shop catalog. A handle that is not in that list is dropped.

Are these gifts for men or women only?

No. The for him, for her and for the home choices are gift lists, not clothing departments. The pieces are unisex unless a product page says otherwise.

Do you promise a holiday arrival date?

No. Pieces are made after you order. The shipping and returns page explains production time, and the estimate for your address is shown at checkout.
