Holiday gift guide

Holiday gifts, Wrightsville first

A short list from the ShoreList shop for a Wilmington Christmas. Wrightsville Beach leads. Every piece is made to order, and this page does not invent a delivery date.

Shipping, without a made-up cutoff

We do not publish a holiday order-by date. Pieces are made after you order. The shipping and returns page says most orders ship within 2 to 7 business days of being submitted, then transit time applies. The estimate for your address is shown at checkout, before you pay.

Carriers publish their own cutoff calendars. Those pages are the source, not this one: USPS holiday shipping, UPS shipping support, and FedEx shipping. Nothing on this guide is a promised arrival date.

If you need something with no parcel, the PDF guides are $14 each and download after checkout. For a typed request, ask Wrighty for gift ideas.
