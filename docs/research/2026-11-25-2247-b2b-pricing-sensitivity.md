## Illustrative five-year sensitivity seed (USD, not forecast)
Average active paying hotels by year: 3, 10, 25, 50, 100. Monthly price 149; variable cost per account/month 35 (hosting, support labor, fees allowance); acquisition/onboarding $300 per gross new account; annual churn assumption 20% of prior average account count; gross additions approximated by current minus previous average plus churn. Fixed annual economic overhead 12000,18000,30000,48000,84000. No other revenue assumed. Formula annual contribution = 12*N*(price-variable) - 300*(N-prevN+0.2*prevN) - fixed. Simplified average-account model, not cohort forecast; taxes, refunds, detailed licensing, seasonality and working capital not separately modeled. Year 1 -8796; year 2 -6720; year 3 -900; year 4 11400; year 5 34800. Year 3 sensitivity: price $99 => -15900; $199 => 14100; variable cost $65 => -9900; acquisition $600 => -6000. Break-even steady-state at year 3 fixed overhead: ceil(30000/(12*(149-35)-300*.2)) = 23 accounts. No evidence yet that ShoreList can acquire these accounts.
