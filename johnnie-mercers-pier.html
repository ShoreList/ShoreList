Wrightsville Beach

The fishing pier at Wrightsville Beach

Johnnie Mercer's Pier is the fishing pier on the ocean side of Wrightsville Beach. This note is the address, how parking is described, and where to open hours, fees, the fishing page and the live cam. ShoreList does not run the pier, does not use its logo, and is not affiliated with it.

The pier's contact page lists 23 E Salisbury St, Wrightsville Beach, NC 28480. Its home page calls it the first concrete fishing pier in North Carolina, in the heart of Wrightsville Beach, extending more than 1,200 feet into the Atlantic. That page also says the concrete pier reopened in 2001 after hurricanes in 1996 damaged the earlier wooden pier. Those sentences are the pier's account, checked on its site on 27 November 2026. The walk out starts at that address and goes over the Atlantic. Banks Channel is the sheltered water on the sound side of the island, not the water under this pier. The Loop at Wrightsville Beach crosses Banks Channel and passes Wrightsville Beach Park, which is the flat way around the middle of the island if you are already on foot.

Where it is

Use the street address above. Wrightsville Beach is the island east of downtown Wilmington, and this pier is on the ocean side of that island. Masonboro Inlet and Masonboro Island are south of the strand. Masonboro Island is undeveloped and reached by boat, so it is not the way onto the pier. For the wider town, see the Wrightsville Beach guide.

Parking

The pier's FAQ says pay parking at the pier and along the side streets is operated by the town of Wrightsville Beach. It lists $6 an hour or $30 for the day, enforced from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., seven days a week, with metered parking from March 1 through October 31. Parking questions, it says, go to the town. Follow the sign at the space you use, because the town can change a rate or a season without this page changing the same day. Beach-wide dates, without those pier rates, are on the Wilmington parking guide.

Hours, fees, fishing and the live cam

Hours and fees stay on the pier's site. The contact page and the FAQ do not state the same Friday and Saturday closing time, so this guide does not pick one and does not copy a fee table. For a fishing report, passes and rod rental, use the fishing page. The live cam is on its own page. Nothing here embeds a camera or a video. The front door of the site is johnniemercersfishingpier.com.
