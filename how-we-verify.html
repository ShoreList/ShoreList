How ShoreList verifies events

Where our event listings come from, how often they refresh, and what to do if something is wrong.

Where listings come from

Event listings come from two places. Some are entered by ShoreList from organizer, venue and town pages. Others are read automatically from public calendar feeds (iCal) that organizers publish themselves.

How often they refresh

A refresh runs once a day. It re-reads the calendar feeds, keeps the listings ShoreList entered by hand, and drops any event whose end date has passed. Calendar-feed events are kept only for the next 120 days. If a feed cannot be reached on a given day, its last known listings stay in place instead of disappearing. After each refresh, the events pages are rebuilt and published automatically.

What verified means here

An event gets its own page only when it points to a source we checked (the “Source we checked” link on that page) or comes straight from the organizer’s own calendar feed. Listings without either stay in the date lists and link to the organizer’s page, but have no page of their own. Verified is not a guarantee: organizers change dates, times and prices, so confirm with the organizer before you go.

Event pages show only details held in our listing data. If a price, address, organizer or photo is not in the data, the page leaves it out rather than guessing.

Report a correction

If a date, time, venue or link is wrong, email rj@shorelist.net with the event name, the page address and, if you have it, a link to the organizer’s page showing the right details.

What we do not do

  • No paid rankings. Events are listed in date order.
  • No fake reviews.
