How the planner works

A plain description, so you know what you are getting.

1

You describe the day

Your taps and any note you add become one short brief of up to 600 characters. That text is the only thing sent to the planner.

2

An AI model drafts it

The model works from a list of experience types on ShoreList, such as ghost tours, film tours, surf lessons and nature walks, and writes four to six timed stops for one day.

3

You check and book

Each stop links to its ShoreList page, where you can see what is bookable now. The planner does not look up availability, prices, tides or opening hours, so confirm before you go.
